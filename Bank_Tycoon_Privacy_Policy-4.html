Bank Tycoon – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bank Tycoon handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Bank Tycoon does not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

Children's Privacy

Bank Tycoon is designed to be suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

If a future version of the game includes advertising, analytics, login services, or other third-party services, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. This policy will be updated when applicable.

Data Security

Because the game does not intentionally collect personal information, we do not maintain a personal-information database for the game.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its services change. Any updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the game developer through the app store or distribution platform where Bank Tycoon is published.
